Welcome to the Boards section of the AgOpenGPS hardware documentation! This area
is designed to help you understand, choose, and work with the various circuit
boards that make AgOpenGPS possible.

## What are "Boards" in AgOpenGPS?

In AgOpenGPS, "boards" refer to the custom or community-designed printed circuit
boards (PCBs) that connect and control the hardware components of your autosteer
or implement control system.

## Types of Boards Covered

- **All-in-One Boards**: The most popular and recommended option for new builds.
  These combine all major functions (steering, GPS, IMU, etc.) onto a single
  board. [See All-in-One Boards](./all-in-one-boards/)
- **Implement Boards**: Boards designed for section control, rate control, or
  other implement-specific tasks. [See Implement Boards](./implement-boards/)
- **Other & Legacy Boards**: Older designs, alternative solutions, and
  enclosures. Useful for reference or special projects.
  [See Other Boards](./other-boards/)
- **Configuring Boards**: Guides for flashing firmware, configuring GPS modules,
  and setting up your system. [See Configuring Boards](./configuring-boards/)

## How to Use This Section

1. **Start with All-in-One Boards** if you are building a new system. These are
   the most supported and easiest to assemble.
2. **Check Implement Boards** if you need section or rate control for
   implements.
3. **Browse Other Boards** for legacy or alternative solutions, including
   enclosures.
4. **Visit Configuring Boards** for step-by-step setup and troubleshooting
   guides.
